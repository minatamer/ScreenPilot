RESPONSIVE & ACCESSIBLE

Same product at every size

The design changes its arrangement, not its meaning. No important content or recovery action disappears on a smaller screen.

Responsive rules

  • Works continuously from 320 px through 2560 px and beyond.
  • No horizontal page scrolling or clipped primary actions.
  • Mobile uses dynamic viewport space and safe-area padding.
  • Entry choices keep equal visual weight when stacked.
  • Captions stay readable and never sit under fixed controls.

Accessibility rules

  • Normal text meets WCAG AA contrast.
  • Keyboard focus is always visible.
  • Controls are at least 44 × 44 px.
  • Status uses words or icons as well as color.
  • No microphone or voice session starts automatically.
  • Captions remain available throughout the call.

Motion: normal transitions last 180–240 ms. Insight cards may enter over 320–450 ms. Corrections use a quiet border/color change—never a shake. Reduced-motion mode removes travel, pulsing, and smooth scrolling.

Reference boundary: the supplied dashboard image influenced only the Deep Ink + Warm Coral palette and warm visual mood. ScreenPilot does not inherit its recruiter navigation, candidate profiles, pipeline, analytics, scoring, phone controls, or other unsupported features.
