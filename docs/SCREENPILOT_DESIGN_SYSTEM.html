RESPONSIVE & ACCESSIBLE

Same product at every size

The design changes its arrangement, not its meaning. No important content or recovery action disappears on a smaller screen.

Responsive rules

  • Works continuously from 320 px through 2560 px and beyond.
  • No horizontal page scrolling or clipped primary actions.
  • Mobile uses dynamic viewport space and safe-area padding.
  • Entry choices keep equal visual weight when stacked.
  • Captions stay readable and never sit under fixed controls.

Accessibility rules

  • Normal text meets WCAG AA contrast.
  • Keyboard focus is always visible.
  • Controls are at least 44 × 44 px.
  • Status uses words or icons as well as color.
  • No microphone or voice session starts automatically.
  • Captions remain available throughout the call.

Motion: normal transitions last 180–240 ms. The hero word “notes.” is a slightly informal handwritten SVG whose individual pen strokes draw over about 1.1 seconds; an imperfect coral underline follows immediately, then the treatment stays still. The ordinary word remains in the accessible headline. Same-page landing CTAs glide smoothly to their targets instead of jumping while ordinary activation keeps the browser address fragment-free; header How it works, hero See it step by step, and the hero entry action share one brisk 0.9-second journey. The landing preview loops slowly through two three-topic boards: salary, experience, and availability, followed by role preference, work setup, and growth goal, then back to the first board. It has no playback/topic controls, pauses on deliberate keyboard/click focus and when off-screen or hidden, and holds each completed board before transitioning. The active speaker bars and participant waveform scale in place, both question and answer subtitles reveal one spoken word at a time inside fixed-height regions, and note boards/cards enter with opacity plus a small transform. The four-step how-it-works scene changes in response to normal scrolling without hijacking it. Reduced-motion mode keeps the completed handwritten word visible and uses only a gentle opacity finish, cycles through complete speaking and note states without word-by-word or automatic spatial movement, and limits voice bars to slow opacity changes; every story step remains readable.

Hero layout: keep the opening permanently stacked and centered. The promise and actions come first, and the wide interview product frame sits directly beneath them at every viewport width—never beside them.

Landing header: keep its surface fully transparent and non-sticky inside the edge-to-edge continuous atmosphere so it scrolls away with the page. Place the enlarged ScreenPilot mark and wordmark at the left and the larger How it works / Start demo action cluster at the right; keyboard order follows the same familiar layout. The mark links cleanly to `/`, How it works shares the hero's fragment-free controlled scroll journey, and Start demo routes directly to the `/demo` entry chooser. The page-wide grid and glow continue behind the header and hero without restarting at their boundary.

Reference boundary: the supplied dashboard image influenced only the Deep Ink + Warm Coral palette and warm visual mood. ScreenPilot does not inherit its recruiter navigation, candidate profiles, pipeline, analytics, scoring, phone controls, or other unsupported features.
